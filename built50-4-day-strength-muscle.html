THE PROGRAM

BUILT50 4-DAY
STRENGTH & MUSCLE.

This is a straightforward upper/lower program for lifters who want strength and muscle without turning every week into a recovery contest. The goal isn't to train less because you're getting older. The goal is to make the work count.

BUILT50 RULE

The goal is not to leave the gym destroyed. The goal is to give your body a reason to adapt — then come back strong enough to do it again.

HOW TO RUN IT

MAKE THE PROGRAM WORK.

1. WARM UP — DON'T WEAR YOURSELF OUT

Start with 5–10 minutes of easy movement if needed, then use progressively heavier warm-up sets before the first major lift. Warm-up sets prepare you for the working weight; they aren't supposed to become another workout.

2. LEAVE A LITTLE IN THE TANK

Most working sets should finish with roughly 1–3 good reps remaining. You can train hard without taking every compound movement to absolute failure.

3. USE DOUBLE PROGRESSION

Start with a weight you can control near the bottom of the listed rep range. Over time, add reps. Once you can reach the top of the range with good form across your working sets, increase the load slightly and build again.

4. DON'T FORCE PAINFUL EXERCISES

The exercise list is a framework, not a loyalty test. If a movement consistently hurts a joint, use a similar movement pattern that you can perform comfortably and progressively.

5. RUN IT FOR 8 WEEKS

Give the program enough time to work. Track your loads and reps for eight weeks instead of changing exercises every time you see a new workout online.

6. DELOAD WHEN YOU ACTUALLY NEED IT

If performance is falling, soreness is lingering, joints are getting increasingly irritated and normal sessions suddenly feel unusually difficult, consider an easier week. Reduce load, sets or both, then build back up.
