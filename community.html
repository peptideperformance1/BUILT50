MORE THAN A COMMENT SECTION

REAL PEOPLE.
REAL CONVERSATIONS.

BUILT50 isn't about pretending everybody trains the same, eats the same or has the same goals.

It's about bringing together people who care about strength, bodybuilding, recovery, nutrition, performance and continuing to improve as life moves forward.

You don't need to be 50. You just need to understand the idea behind BUILT50: getting older is mandatory — getting weaker isn't.
